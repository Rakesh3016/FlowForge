Objective

Blue: carry live water from any bottom source to the orange jar at the top center.

Orange: carry fire from any top source to the blue jar at the bottom center.

First to deliver 12 units into the opponent's jar wins. Flow advances one pipe tile per own turn. Disconnected pipes spill; repaired pipes refill gradually. Two or more wet sources deliver up to 2 units per turn.

Controls

  1. Tap one of your six generator ports or a generator on the board. If it has only one legal build or movement option, that move happens immediately—no second tap.
  2. Tap a green outlined adjacent tile to build one pipe and move your generator.
  3. Tap a red outlined adjacent enemy tile to attack. Destroying any enemy pipe, including an empty one, forces your attacker to advance onto and claim the tile, exposing it to nearby enemy support.
  4. Tap a reachable friendly pipe to travel along your network in one turn.
  5. Your last-used generator stays selected on your next turn, with all legal moves highlighted.
  6. Every generator starts as 1 (Gen1). Select a Gen1, tap Merge Gen1 + Gen1, then tap a purple-outlined Gen1 reachable through your pipes to form 2 (Gen2).
  7. Attacking Gen2 only downgrades it to Gen1, so the attacker stays put. Destroying an unoccupied enemy pipe or killing Gen1 captures its tile. Counterattacks happen on the opponent's next turn, not automatically.
  8. Flow advances one tile per own turn. When disconnected, upper segments spill and refill one tile at a time after repairs. The jar only scores after the flow arrives.

When not in Merge mode, tapping another friendly generator selects it. Merge consumes one turn. For this prototype, neighboring friendly pipes connect automatically. It has no obstacles, special tiles, pipe rotations, or pressure chambers.
